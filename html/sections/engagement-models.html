Our Engagement Models

Three clear ways to work with us — pick based on scope certainty, delivery speed, and how much you expect requirements to change.

💸

Fixed Cost Model

  1. Locked scope. Requirements are defined upfront with clear acceptance criteria.
  2. Predictable budget. You know total cost before build starts.
  3. Milestone delivery. Work is shipped in stages with sign-offs.
  4. Schedule clarity. Timeline is planned around agreed deliverables.
  5. Risk control. Ideal when you want minimum change during execution.
⏳

Time & Material Model

  1. Built for iteration. Perfect when scope evolves after real usage feedback.
  2. Pay for output time. Billing maps directly to engineering effort spent.
  3. Scale on demand. Ramp team up/down as priorities shift.
  4. High visibility. Weekly updates, clear time tracking, and delivery demos.
  5. Fast decisions. Best when speed matters more than rigid planning.
🤝

IT Staff Augmentation

  1. Add capacity quickly. Plug vetted engineers into your team and workflows.
  2. Transparent tracking. Hours, tasks, and progress are always visible.
  3. Longer engagements. Best for ongoing delivery and platform continuity.
  4. Flexible roles. DevOps, backend, frontend, QA — based on your gap.
  5. Ownership stays with you. You lead priorities; we execute and ship.
